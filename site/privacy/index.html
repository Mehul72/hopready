HopReady privacy policy

Draft. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Before publishing: the publisher must confirm a contact email and review this policy against the released build.

The short version

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your saved places, recent searches, preferences and parked car stay on your iPhone.
  • To plan a trip, the places involved are sent to HopReady's server, which asks Transport for NSW and Apple Maps for routes. HopReady's server does not keep them after answering.

What stays on your iPhone

Home and work, favourite places and routes, recent searches, trip preferences, the location of your parked car, parking spots you saved, the trip you are on, and a random install identifier. This is stored in the app's own storage and is never uploaded as a set. Settings > Delete all HopReady data erases it, as does deleting the app.

What is sent, and why

When What is sent Who receives it
You plan a trip Start, destination and (for a trip home) parked-car locations, the time, and your trip preferences HopReady's server, which sends locations and times to Transport for NSW (public transport) and Apple Maps (drive times)
You search for a place What you type, and your approximate location if you allowed location access HopReady's server, which asks Transport for NSW (stops) and Apple Maps (places)
You check car parks Nothing personal HopReady's server and Transport for NSW
Every request The random install identifier, and your IP address as part of the connection HopReady's server, to limit abuse. Not passed to Transport for NSW or Apple
You tap Start driving The destination of the drive The navigation app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze)

HopReady asks for your agreement before the first live search sends any of this. Demo mode sends nothing.

Location

HopReady only uses your location while the app is open, and only after you tap Use current location and allow it. It never tracks you in the background. You can always type a start point instead.

What HopReady's server keeps

HopReady's server runs on Cloudflare Workers. It answers each request and does not store locations, addresses or search text. Its logs record technical details only, such as which feature was used, how long it took and whether a provider failed. They never include locations, addresses, search text or keys. Cloudflare keeps these logs for a few days. Rate limits briefly count requests per install identifier and per IP address.

Transport for NSW and Apple

Transport for NSW and Apple receive locations and search text from HopReady's server, not from your phone, and without your identity or install identifier. They handle requests under their own terms and privacy policies: Transport for NSW privacy and Apple privacy. Maps shown in the app come from Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

HopReady is not directed at children under 13.

HopReady privacy policy

Draft. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Before publishing: the publisher must confirm a contact email and review this policy against the released build.

The short version

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your saved places, recent searches, preferences and parked car stay on your iPhone.
  • To plan a trip, the places involved are sent to HopReady's server, which asks Transport for NSW and Apple Maps for routes. HopReady's server does not keep them after answering.
  • HopReady asks before the first live request. Demo mode sends nothing to its server.

What stays on your iPhone

Home and work, favourite places and routes, recent searches, trip preferences, the location of your parked car, parking spots you saved, the trip you are on, and a random install identifier. This is stored in the app's own storage. Parts of it leave the phone only inside a request you make, as listed below, and never as a set. Settings > Delete all HopReady data erases it, as does deleting the app.

What is sent, and why

When What is sent Who receives it
You plan a trip Start and destination, the time and your trip preferences. With "I'll drive and park", also the parking spots you saved (their names and positions). For Take me home, where your car is. HopReady's server, which sends positions and times, not names, to Transport for NSW (public transport) and Apple Maps (drive times)
You search for a place What you type and, if you have allowed location access, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) so nearby places come first HopReady's server, which asks Transport for NSW (stops) and Apple Maps (places)
You use your current location, in live mode Your position Apple, from your phone through iOS, to look up the street name. Demo mode skips this
The app shows a map The area on screen Apple Maps, from your phone, to draw the map
You check car parks Nothing personal HopReady's server and Transport for NSW
Every request The random install identifier, and your IP address as part of the connection HopReady's server, to limit abuse. Not passed to Transport for NSW or Apple
You tap Start driving The destination of the drive The navigation app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze)

HopReady asks for your agreement before the first live request sends any of this. Demo mode sends nothing to HopReady's server; its maps still come from Apple.

Location

HopReady uses your location only while the app is open, and only after you allow it. It reads your position when you tap Use current location, and, once you have allowed access, to show nearby places first when you search. It never tracks you in the background. You can always type a start point instead.

What HopReady's server keeps

HopReady's server runs on Cloudflare Workers. It answers each request and does not store locations, addresses or search text. Its logs record technical details only, such as which feature was used, how long it took and whether a provider failed. They never include locations, addresses, search text or keys. Cloudflare keeps these logs for a few days. Rate limits briefly count requests per install identifier and per IP address.

Transport for NSW and Apple

Transport for NSW and Apple receive locations and search text from HopReady's server, without your identity or install identifier. Apple also receives requests straight from your phone to draw maps and, in live mode, to name the street of your current location. HopReady shares data only with services whose privacy commitments protect it at least as well as this policy: Transport for NSW, Apple, and Cloudflare, which runs HopReady's server.

Your choices

  • Settings > Live data use shows what is sent and lets you withdraw your agreement. Live trips then stop until you agree again. Demo mode keeps working.
  • Settings > Delete all HopReady data erases everything HopReady stored on your iPhone, including the agreement.
  • Location access can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

HopReady is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the app update that needs it.

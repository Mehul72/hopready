Where HopReady works

Greater Sydney, including the Central Coast and Blue Mountains. Trips that start or end outside that area are not planned.

How accurate are the times?

Public transport times come from Transport for NSW. A time marked Live uses real-time information; one marked Timetable is the schedule. Drive times are Apple Maps estimates for the time you travel. Parking buffers, transfer buffers and walking limits are yours to set in Settings. Plans can change: refresh before you leave.

Car park spaces

Space counts come from Transport for NSW's car park feed. They are estimates of spaces free right now, shown with how old they are. They do not guarantee a space when you arrive. Parking rules (for example the 18-hour free limit with an Opal tap on and off) are shown separately, with a link to the official page.

Getting home to your car

A suggested car park is not proof of where you parked. After you park, tap I parked here or confirm your car's location on the Car tab. Take me home then plans public transport back to that exact spot, then the drive home. If you were dropped off, choose I was dropped off and HopReady plans public transport home instead.

Using HopReady without location access

Location is optional. Type your start point instead. iOS asks for permission the first time you ask HopReady to start from where you are, for example with Use current location, Take me home or a favourite in Saved. HopReady reads your location only while you are using the app.

What HopReady sends

The first time you use live trips, HopReady explains what it sends (the places you search for and travel between) and asks you to agree. Settings > Live data use shows it again and lets you withdraw. The privacy policy has the details.

Demo mode

Demo mode shows sample trips from built-in data so you can try HopReady anywhere. It is labelled on every screen and is never used for real trips. Turn it on or off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Your favourites, recent searches, preferences and parked car are stored only on your iPhone. Settings > Delete all HopReady data removes all of it. Deleting the app does too.
